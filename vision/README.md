# `vision/` — the product we intend, not the system that exists

> **Membership test**
> "This describes the product we **intend**, not the system that exists."

**Lifecycle: living, and deliberately aspirational.** This is the one bucket where a gap between the
document and reality is not a bug — it is the point. Everything here describes a future.

That is exactly why it must not sit in [`docs/`](../docs/), whose contract is the opposite. Mixing
the two is how a roadmap gets read as a spec and someone builds a feature nobody committed to.

## What belongs here

PR-FAQs · positioning and narrative · founding notes · strategy memos · the pitch · "what this
becomes in two years."

## What does not

| Not this | Goes to |
|---|---|
| What the system does today | [`docs/`](../docs/) |
| A dated decision about direction | [`decisions/`](../decisions/) |
| A prioritized backlog being executed | `tasks.md`, or a tracker |

## For agents

Treat everything here as **intent, never as specification**. A PR-FAQ describing a feature is not
evidence the feature exists, and must never be cited as a description of current behavior. When
vision and `docs/` disagree, `docs/` describes reality.
